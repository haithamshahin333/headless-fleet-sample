The portal is installed as {{ include "portal.fullname" . }} in namespace {{ .Release.Namespace }}.

1. Register this redirect URI on the portal's app in your identity provider
   (exactly as shown, with no trailing slash):

     {{ include "portal.url" . }}/api/auth/callback/oidc

2. Check LangSmith, the identity provider and the portal from inside the cluster:

     helm test {{ .Release.Name }} -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} --logs

3. Open {{ include "portal.url" . }} and sign in.

     kubectl logs -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} deploy/{{ include "portal.fullname" . }}
